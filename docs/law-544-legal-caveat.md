# Law 544/2001 Legal Caveat

This repository is an educational and civic-technology demo. It is not an
official Romanian public administration service and does not create, submit, or
resolve legal requests under Law 544/2001.

## What The Demo Models

- a public information request moving through administrative states
- registry assignment
- director routing
- public servant processing
- document attachment and final response hashing
- public audit visibility for state transitions

## What Requires Legal Review

Before any production use, the workflow would need Romanian legal review for:

- exact Law 544/2001 deadlines and exception handling
- institution-specific registry obligations
- response refusal categories and appeal paths
- archival, retention, and deletion obligations
- qualified electronic signature requirements
- public/private field separation
- accessibility and language requirements for citizens

## Demo Interpretation

The demo uses simplified state names and role labels. They are useful for
explaining a bureaucracy-as-code pattern, but they should not be treated as
authoritative legal taxonomy.
